Grouping rules

Your category rules

Assign a site to a category. Your rules win over the built-in list. Type a new name to make your own category. A rule like google.com also covers mail.google.com, and google.com/travel covers just that section.

    Never group these sites

    Tabs from these sites always stay out of groups. Pinned tabs are never grouped either.

      Built-in categories

      Uncheck a category to stop using it. Open one to see the sites it covers.
