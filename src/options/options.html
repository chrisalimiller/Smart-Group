Smart Group settings

Grouping rules

Every other tab is sorted into a category. Tabs that don't fit any category go into a "Miscellaneous" group.

Your category rules

Assign a site to a category. Your rules win over the built-in list. Type a new name to make your own category. Dragging a tab into one of Smart Group's category groups adds a rule here for that tab's site. A rule like google.com also covers mail.google.com, and google.com/travel covers just that section.

    Never group these sites

    Tabs from these sites always stay out of groups. Pinned tabs are never grouped either.

      Built-in categories

      Uncheck a category to stop using it (its tabs go to Miscellaneous instead). Open one to see the sites it covers. Sites not on any list are matched by words in their address or page title.
